Delete your Petlife account

This page explains how to delete your Petlife account and what happens to your data.

In the app

  1. Open Petlife and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap the gear (Settings).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, tap "Yes, continue", type DELETE and tap "Delete my account".

Your account is deleted straight away. There is no waiting period and it cannot be undone. We send a confirmation email when it is done.

You can't delete your account while a booking is in progress or a safety report you are part of is still open. The app tells you which. Finish the booking, or wait for our team to close the report, then try again.

Without the app

Email support@petlifeindia.co from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm it is you.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, name, mobile number, email address and photos
  • Your pets and their records
  • Your saved addresses and location data
  • Your chats
  • Your emergency contacts
  • Your ID verification records and professional documents (Pet Buddies)

Copies in our backups are deleted within 30 days.

What is kept

A booking belongs to both people on it. The other person keeps the booking, the ratings on it and any safety report, shown as "Deleted user". Your name, contact details, address, notes and location are removed from it. Bookings that have not happened yet are cancelled, and the other person is told.

We keep a record of who messaged whom and when, not what was said, for one year, so we can investigate safety reports and disputes between users.
