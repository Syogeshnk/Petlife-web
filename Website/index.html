Good questions

Before you ask…

How are Pet Buddies verified?›

Every Pet Buddy is verified using official government identity documents, Aadhaar or an equivalent government-issued ID, which our team checks before their profile goes live. Their phone number is verified over WhatsApp, they complete a full profile with photos and working hours, and they build a public review history. Vets and trainers can add professional credentials, which we verify separately. No verified ID means no listing on Petlife.

What does Petlife charge?›

Nothing. Petlife is purely a community platform that connects Pet Parents directly with verified local Pet Buddies. We charge 0% commission and zero hidden fees: no booking charge, no platform fee, no surge pricing. You agree a price with your Pet Buddy and pay them directly, in full.

When is the app launching?›

The Petlife app is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Pet Buddies can apply today using the form above so their profile is verified and ready on day one. Follow @petlife_india for the launch date.

Which cities is Petlife in?›

We're growing neighbourhood by neighbourhood across India. Our largest communities are Pune, Mumbai and Bengaluru, and we're also in Mangaluru, Nashik, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad and Chennai. Open the app and it will tell you exactly who's near you.

Can I adopt a pet through Petlife?›

Yes. Our Adopt a Pet listings bring together indies, rescues and rehomed pets from verified shelters and foster homes near you, so you can meet them before you commit.

Can I be both a Pet Parent and a Pet Buddy?›

Yes, it's one account with two modes. Switch anytime from your profile and the whole app adapts to whichever hat you're wearing.
