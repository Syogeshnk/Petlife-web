4. What verification actually means

Before a Pet Buddy profile goes live we check an official government identity document and verify their phone number over WhatsApp. Where someone claims a professional credential, we ask to see it.

Such verification confirms identity alone. It affords no indication of competence, character or standard of service.

A verification badge does not constitute, and shall not be construed or relied upon as constituting, an endorsement, recommendation, accreditation, criminal antecedent verification, guarantee of competence or assurance of outcome. The Company undertakes no continuous or periodic monitoring of Pet Buddies.

MANDATORY PRE-SERVICE VERIFICATION BY THE PET PARENT. Notwithstanding any verification attempted by the Company, the ultimate and non-delegable onus rests upon the Pet Parent independently to verify, audit, interrogate and exercise due diligence in respect of the credentialing, qualification, licensing, competence, character, suitability, insurance and background of any Pet Buddy prior to accepting or permitting the rendering of any Service. See Clause 5 of the Terms & Conditions.

Reviews are the personal opinions of the people who wrote them. We remove reviews that break our rules or the law, but we do not edit them to be more flattering, and we do not accept payment to remove or promote a review.

8. Measures operated by the Company

The foregoing disclaimers notwithstanding, the Company operates the following measures within the confines of its role as intermediary. None of the following shall be construed as assuming any duty of care, supervision, intervention or guarantee:

  • verify a government identity document and phone number for every Pet Buddy before their profile goes live;
  • use a spoken one-time code at the start of each booking, so you can confirm the right person is at your door;
  • run two-way reviews that stay sealed until both sides submit, so honest feedback is not punished;
  • keep exact addresses private until a booking is confirmed, and blur map locations by roughly 300 metres;
  • investigate reports at our discretion, and suspend, revoke or delete accounts pursuant to Clause 16 of the Terms & Conditions;
