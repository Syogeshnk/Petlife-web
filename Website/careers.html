How we work

Three things we actually mean.

Care

Every decision gets asked one question first: is this better for the pet? Growth targets do not get to overrule that.

Love

We build for the 62-year-old booking a walk for her indie, not just the app-native 25-year-old. If it isn't obvious, it isn't done.

Trust

Pet Parents hand us their family. We verify every Buddy against government ID and never sell data. Not to anyone, ever.
