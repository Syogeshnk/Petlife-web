This policy explains what Petlife collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone using the app, as a Pet Parent or a Pet Buddy.

1. What Data We Collect

  • Account data: your full name, mobile number, email address, and (for Pet Buddies) a service area. If you choose Sign in with Google, we receive the name and email address of your Google account. We never collect payment cards, UPI IDs or other payment details: no payments happen inside the app.
  • Pet data: species, breed, age, vaccination and health notes, and photos you add. Pet insurance details are optional and never required to book.
  • Location data: your precise location, only while you use the app and only if you allow it: to find Pet Buddies near you, to set your neighbourhood, at the start of a booking, when you share your location in a chat, and if you raise an SOS. We never ask for background location permission. For Pet Buddies only, live location during an active booking continues while the app is in the background, with a notification on the Pet Buddy's phone the whole time (see section 3).
  • Addresses: addresses you save, and the address of each booking with its map pin. The address is shared with the Pet Buddy only after they accept the booking.
  • Emergency contacts: up to three people you add, with their name, mobile number and, if you give it, email address. Please tell them first. We contact them by WhatsApp or email only in an SOS: when you raise one, or, if you are a pet parent, when your Pet Buddy reports that your pet is in danger. We tell them who raised it, never your location, your booking details or anyone's number. Their details are deleted when you remove them or delete your account. A contact who wants their details removed can write to support@petlifeindia.co.
  • Session records: booking start and completion code checks (timestamps and pass/fail, never the codes themselves in readable form) and any photos shared during a session.
  • Reviews and ratings: after a booking, each side can rate the other. Stars, comments and any review photo are shown on the profile of the person rated.
  • Device and security data: a one-way hash of a device identifier and your push notification token, to send you notifications and prevent abuse; a count of failed sign-in attempts, to lock an account under attack and tell you; and crash reports and performance data from the app (technical device data only).
  • Lost pet alerts and adoption posts: if you report a lost pet or post a pet for adoption, the details you add (such as the pet's name, photo and approximate area) are shown to app users nearby, never an exact address. Alerts about lost pets near you are sent only if you turn them on.
  • Government ID verification (Pet Buddies only): you upload a government ID and a selfie, and a trained reviewer at Petlife checks them by hand. While the review is open, the images and any PAN or Driving Licence number are kept in private, encrypted storage that no other user can reach. Your Aadhaar number is never stored as text beyond its last 4 digits, although the photo of the card shows the full number. As soon as the review is decided, approved or rejected, we delete the document images, the selfie and the full PAN or Driving Licence number. We keep only the last 4 digits of the document number and the record of the decision (who decided, when, and the outcome). Every time a reviewer opens a document or reveals a number, that is recorded.
  • Professional documents (Pet Buddies only): certificates you add as proof of training, a vet registration certificate, and for Pet Taxi your vehicle documents and licence expiry date. They are kept while your account exists, so your profile can show them and we can remind you before they expire, and are deleted with your account.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to run the marketplace: showing Pet Buddies to nearby pet parents, verifying identity, keeping sessions safe, sending booking notices and reminders (including your pet's vaccination and health reminders), and keeping an audit trail in case something goes wrong. We send marketing messages only if you opt in, under Settings → Consent & privacy; it is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time. We do not sell your data, or your pet's, to anyone, and we do not share it for advertising.

3. GPS Location Data

We collect your GPS coordinates when you use the location feature. Your exact coordinates are stored securely and never shown to any other user, except a Pet Buddy's live location during an active booking (below). For Pet Buddies, only the neighbourhood name (e.g. 'Koramangala') and a rough map position, within about 300 metres, are shown publicly. Pet parent locations are never shown publicly at all. You can remove your location data by updating your profile or deleting your account.

During an active booking, a Pet Buddy's live location is additionally shared, in real time, only with the pet parent on that one booking. It starts when the session starts (the start code, or the Pet Buddy's Start button) and stops when the session ends or the booking is cancelled. While it runs, the Pet Buddy's phone shows a notification; it is never active outside an in-progress booking. The live stream is not stored; only a sparse, sampled trail (roughly one point every few minutes) is kept afterward for safety and dispute resolution, for 90 days after the booking ends, and is then deleted.

If you raise an SOS, your location at that moment goes to Petlife's safety team. The other person on the booking is told an SOS was raised. Your emergency contacts are told who raised it, never your location.

4. Who We Share Data With

The other party on your booking sees what they need to provide or receive the service: your pet's profile, the booking address, and your chosen contact channel, only after a booking exists, never before.

We also use a small set of processors, each only for the service it provides: Supabase (our database, authentication and file storage, hosted in the ap-south-1 region in Mumbai, India, so your data is stored in India); Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API (verification and password-reset codes, booking and SOS notices); Resend (emails, including SOS emails to emergency contacts); Expo and Google Firebase (push notifications); Google (Sign in with Google if you use it, map rendering, and route distance for Pet Taxi); OpenStreetMap Nominatim (turning a location into an area name); and Sentry (crash and performance reports: technical device data only, never your profile, messages or location history).

Session records may be shared with law enforcement in response to a valid legal request, or used internally to resolve a dispute between the two parties on a booking.

5. Data Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account is immediate and permanent: there is no waiting period, no cancel window, and nothing can be recovered afterwards. It removes your profile, pets, photos, contact details, chats, emergency contacts, ID records and professional documents. Copies in our backups are deleted within 30 days.

A booking belongs to both people on it. When you delete your account, the other person keeps the booking, the ratings on it and any safety report, shown as 'Deleted user'; your name, contact details, address, notes and location are removed from them. Bookings that have not happened yet are cancelled, and the other person is told. You cannot delete your account while a booking is in progress or a safety report you are part of is open.

Chat messages attached to a booking are deleted when that booking finishes or is cancelled. Messages that are not attached to a booking are deleted after 30 days. Photos and videos sent in chat go with the message they belong to. We keep a record of who messaged whom and when, not what was said, for one year, so we can investigate safety reports and disputes between users. Nobody at Petlife reads it in the ordinary course.

Two exceptions. If an SOS is raised during a booking, nothing in that conversation is deleted while it is open; deletion runs once it is closed. If a safety incident is reported during a booking and is still open when the booking ends, the conversation is kept, read-only, until our team closes the incident or for at most 90 days after the booking ends, and is then deleted.

6. Message Encryption

Messages you send in chat are encrypted on your device whenever both devices can support it. When a message is encrypted, it can only be opened on your device and the device of the person you are talking to. We cannot read it, and we hold no key that would let us: there is no master key, and no way for us to recover it if you lose access.

Encryption is not always possible. If the other person has not yet set up encryption on their device, or if your own device gives us nowhere secure to keep a key, the message is sent unencrypted, and in that case we do hold it and could read it. When that happens, the conversation tells you so at the top of the thread, every time, for as long as it lasts.

Two honest consequences. If you reinstall the app, past messages cannot be recovered, though messages are deleted when a booking completes anyway, so there is rarely more than a few days to lose. And if you sign in on a second device, that device sees only messages sent after it.

We do not ask you to compare security codes with the other person. Both of you are identity-verified by Petlife, so what this protects against is someone gaining access to our servers, not us pretending to be one of you.

One thing is deliberately not encrypted: if you raise an SOS during a booking and choose to share the conversation as evidence, your own device decrypts it and uploads it. That only ever happens when you tap the button that says so.

7. Your Rights

You can access and correct your data any time from your profile. You can ask for a copy of your data from Settings → Get a copy of your data; we check the request, prepare the file, and the download link works for a limited time. You can delete your account (and its data) from Settings → Delete Account in the app, or by emailing support@petlifeindia.co from the email address on your account; we reply to a deletion request within 30 days. The steps are also at petlifeindia.co/delete-account. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you also have the right to grievance redressal.

Grievance Officer (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Information Technology Rules, 2021): email info@petlifeindia.co. We acknowledge every grievance within 24 hours and aim to resolve it within 15 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Cookies and Storage

Petlife is a mobile app, not a website: there are no browser cookies and no third-party advertising trackers. Your sign-in session is stored on your device in encrypted storage.

9. Children's Privacy

Petlife is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we terminate accounts found to belong to minors. Content involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors is removed and reported to the authorities.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. For significant changes we will tell you in the app.
